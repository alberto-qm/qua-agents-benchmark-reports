Valid RB counts every RB fit that saw at least one decay length, whether or not the calibration behind it was meaningful. It includes n12's gilboa qC3 (1.02 %) and qolab Q6 (16.0 %), and n13's arbel qA5 (9.4 %). Wall and QPU come from each cell's own clock. The total run is the scheduler's first launch to its "all 37 cells finished" line. Cost is tinycal's own estimate, summed from the result documents.

Straight through means the agent contributed nothing: the cell completed; every graph node ran exactly once (the power sweep twice, as the recipe repeats it at the sweet spot) and no node run failed or was refused; and every value written was the latest proposal for its path. Numbers count within 1×10−4 relative, because the model retypes and rounds. No write went to a path no node had proposed, and every proposal was written unless it was a no-op. All of it is computed from each cell's events.jsonl.

How edge cases were treated. A run that failed with IQCC's 503 Service Unavailable or an HTTP read timeout is not counted as a run (n12 33, n13 21). Neither is a malformed call with no node name, which runs nothing (n12 5, n13 5). A write the harness refused still counts as a write off the proposal. A node that fails on purpose with retry_parameters still makes its retry a rerun: n13's 02c retries on qolab Q1, Q2, Q3, Q6 disqualify those cells. Model parameters on a run_node call are not part of the definition; graph presets never count. By the stricter test, no parameters of its own, none qualifies: n13's one straight-through cell, arbel qC1, passed its own on 14 of 19 runs (drive_amplitude, flux_span, num_shots, use_state_discrimination). Of the n13 cells, 6 ran every node once without a failed run, and 1 wrote exactly what the nodes proposed (n12: 0 and 3). The usual miss is a write no node proposed. In 27 n13 bring-ups that is 03b's curvature, which the agent copies into freq_vs_flux_01_quad_term before ramsey_vs_flux proposes it.

Decisions needed, and follow-ups

The benchmark reference for qubits parked off their sweet spot

The lab parks aqD1 at 0.227 V, 197 mV from its arc maximum, and aqA6 below its upper sweet spot. The recipe biases at the arc maximum, so the agent's f_01 lands +368.5 and +234.2 MHz from the lab's. The judge gives both 2/4, missing f_01 and the readout amplitude (1.78× and 3.09× the lab's). qD1 is not a bad qubit. It measured RB 0.152 % per Clifford, while the lab's file carries 96.1 % gate fidelity. Options: grade f_01 at the committed flux, or keep the lab's flux offset through the scramble. Either way, the matrix's "bad qubit" label on qD1 is wrong.

02c retries on qolab's amplified flux ports

02c's retry window is capped by the port class, ±2.5 V on qolab's amplified LF-FEM ports, and can extend up to 1.5 V per retry. The largest replayed retry reached −2.2 V (Q2). In n13 four qolab qubits took the retry: Q1 −0.65..+1.65 V, Q2 −1.61..+0.50 V, Q3 −0.50..+1.87 V, Q6 −0.50..+1.56 V, the widest at 1.87 V.
