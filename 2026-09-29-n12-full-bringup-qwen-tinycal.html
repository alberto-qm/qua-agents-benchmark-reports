Readout power optimization's 0.5–3.5× sweep settles at 1.6–3× the lab's amplitude, usually with higher fidelity on qolab, not always on arbel (qB2: 95.3 % at 2.8×).
  • Upper sweet spots. arbel qA6 and qB3 were again calibrated at their upper sweet spots, +234.2 and +33.5 MHz from where the lab parks them; the judge flags f₀₁ on both.
  • Poor every night. gilboa qC1 (RB 1.6 → 3.0 %, DRAG never converged tonight), qC4 (1.9 → 2.4 %) and qD2 (T1 about 1 µs, 4.8 → 8.5 %).
  • RB validity. Every completion tonight has a fit over at least one decay length, against 28 of 33 on n10+n11. gilboa qD3's 0.368 % covers 15 decay lengths; n10's lower 0.179 % was extrapolated from 1.8. qolab Q2 now has a valid 0.119 % (n10's 0.030 % was under one decay length) but sits 0.05 V off the lab's joint offset, f₀₁ -6.9 MHz.
  • Agent choices that cost QPU. qolab Q1 took 4.8 min of QPU: the agent overrode the graph's active reset with thermal on error amplification, DRAG (4 runs, 91 s) and RB. qolab Q3's f₀₁ is -24.46 MHz from the lab's every night; the lab's value is the suspect one.
