State Field Allowlist

From 2 Oct the benchmark's scramble accounts for every field of a pulled IQCC state: each one is kept, scrambled, reset or removed by a rule in workloads/decalibrate_chip.yaml, and a field no rule covers stops the scramble. Before, every field the edit table did not name reached the agent with the lab's value in it.

What leaked, and who read it

  • extras.target_RF_frequency: the lab's f₀₁ within 0.3 MHz on arbel qA6, qB3, qD1, unscrambled since 14 Sep. IQCC's own flux node treats it as “park this qubit here” (iqcc-calibration · 09_ramsey_vs_flux_calibration), and their 6 Sep state flagged the same three at_sweet_spot: False. Read in two n14 and two n15 runs.
  • resonator.extras.f_01_ground / f_01_excited: the lab's readout-resonator frequency, which the judge grades to ±1 MHz, on 19–20 arbel qubits since 30 Sep. Read in one n14 and three n15 runs.

They misled as well as leaked. n15 qC5 took the resonator value for the qubit:

“the resonator extras in the state show f_01_ground: 7.661 GHz and f_01_excited: 7.660 GHz, confirming the qubit IS at ~7.66 GHz, right next to the resonator”

n15 qD1 centred its search on target_RF_frequency, 368 MHz below the sweet spot it had just biased to, and never found its line.

What the scramble does now

  • Every leaf of state.json and wiring.json must match one of 121 rules (61 keep, 9 scramble, 39 reset, 12 remove); the first match decides. An unmatched field refuses the scramble and is named, in --dry-run too.
  • The rules and the edit table are checked against each other: every field the table edits must be named by a rule, and no rule may name a field the table does not edit.
  • Removals and resets are applied before Quam loads the state; a removal that would leave a reference dangling is refused. state_fields.json, written beside edit_plan.json, lists every node removed, reset and scaled.
  • The spec hash moved from f47053f145f74c8c to 252e28f8ab313894, so the judge refuses to compare a run scrambled before with one scrambled after. Earlier arbel runs started with the leaks above in their state.

Still open

8 rules are judgement calls, marked open call below. Two of them bear on what is measured:

The off-sweet-spot qubits. Removing target_RF_frequency stops the leak, but the recipe still sends qA6, qB3 and qD1 to their sweet spots while the judge grades them at the frequency IQCC parks them at. A correct calibration fails the f₀₁ window on all three. That is a judge decision for those three qubits, which the allowlist leaves open.
Pairs and CZ pulses kept wholesale. /qubit_pairs/** and /qubits/*/z/operations/** pass whole, apart from fidelity and confusion records. Nothing in the 1Q bring-up reads or grades them, but a ** rule admits new fields there unreviewed: gilboa's 100 CZ pulse fields of 9 Sep would have passed.
What no allowlist catches: a field whose meaning changes under the same name. n14's arbel depletion_time, sized for the self-depleting Drachma pulse and used with the square one, was that kind; it is handled by the square-readout prep.
